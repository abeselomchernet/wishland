Current platform

Enawuga

A governed shared agent-infrastructure platform designed so licensed institutions and authorized ecosystem partners can bring their own products, accounts, rails, policies and authority into a common operating environment.

The architecture separates service, capability, route and adapter so new partners can be introduced through configuration and certified integration components rather than institution-specific application forks.

01Partner serviceInstitution-specific product or workflow
02Governed capabilityRole, scope, evidence and policy checks
03Approved routeEnvironment-aware route selection
04Certified adapterProtocol-specific partner/provider integration

Role-based operating surfaces

One governed backend, purpose-built workspaces.

Each role sees the information and actions needed for its work. UI visibility never replaces server-side authorization.

What we build

Infrastructure for difficult operating environments.

Our work focuses on reusable infrastructure patterns rather than one-off institutional applications.

Partner integration

Capability contracts, service catalogs, route resolution, reviewed adapters and evidence-gated onboarding.

Offline-resilient operations

Payments & reconciliation

Provider-specific integration foundations with strict separation between provider acceptance and PartnerFI settlement.

Network liquidity decision support

Cash Fleet planning

Simulation and planning support for mobile liquidity operations without implying cash custody, dispatch authority or settlement.

FMCG & logistics coordination

Catalog, inventory and restock planning capabilities bounded from financial authority and settlement.

Governance & evidence

Claims are scoped to evidence.

We distinguish implementation, environment, authorization and production readiness. A deployed component or successful sandbox call does not create regulated authority.
