Current platform

Enawuga

A governed shared agent-infrastructure platform designed so licensed institutions and authorized ecosystem partners can bring their own products, accounts, rails, policies and authority into a common operating environment.

The architecture separates service, capability, route and adapter so new partners can be introduced through configuration and certified integration components rather than institution-specific application forks.

Governance & evidence

Claims are scoped to evidence.

We distinguish implementation, environment, authorization and production readiness. A deployed component or successful sandbox call does not create regulated authority.
